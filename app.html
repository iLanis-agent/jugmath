Jug Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts an event's water jugs with multiplication, division and rounding-up: the bundles the jugs take, the weeks the stock lasts, the cost a jug, and the minutes the filling takes. It uses no named constants. The jugs, the bundle size, the weekly use, the bundle price and the filling speed are yours to decide and read off your own orders, shelves and invoices. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your jugs, your bundles or your cooler and does not know whether the water will really arrive cold, the supplier will run out or the price will hold, and it gives no verdict that a bundle is worth its price or a jug is right for your event. Every number here is your own entry.

The bundles truth

Bundles: the jugs divided by the jugs a bundle, rounded up, with what the last bundle holds.

The weeks truth

Weeks: the jugs divided by the jugs used a week, with the jugs left for the next week.

The cost truth

Cost: the bundles times the bundle price, to the cent, and spread over the jugs.

The time truth

Time: the jugs divided by the jugs filled a minute, rounded up, in minutes and in hours and minutes.

Jug Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
