Jug Math

The 520 jugs at 6 jugs a bundle take 87 bundles, the last bundle holding 4 jugs.

Boundary: arithmetic only Jug Math counts an event's water jugs with multiplication, division and rounding-up: the bundles the jugs take, the weeks the stock lasts, the cost a jug, and the minutes the filling takes. It uses no named constants. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your jugs, your bundles or your cooler and does not know whether the water will really arrive cold, the supplier will run out or the price will hold, and it gives no verdict that a bundle is worth its price or a jug is right for your event. Every number here is your own entry.

Starting examples: 520 jugs, 6 jugs a bundle, 45 jugs a week, $18.50 a bundle and 8 jugs a minute.

The weeks truth

The 520 jugs at 45 jugs a week last 11 full weeks with 25 jugs left for the next week.

The cost truth

The 87 bundles at $18.50 a bundle come to $1609.50, $3.10 a jug.

The time truth

The 520 jugs at 8 jugs a minute take 65 minutes, 1 hour 5 minutes.
